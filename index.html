What we do

Three things, woven into one hour.

Every Sol Flow event moves through the same three elements. How long we spend in each one depends on your group. A team that's been in meetings all day needs something different from a bridal party on day two.

Stillness

Meditation

Anna leads with a dharma talk, some breathwork, and a guided meditation. Ten minutes to help everyone settle into the space before we move.

Movement

Flow

An all-levels vinyasa or yin sequence built for the space. Megan reads the group and adjusts the pace live, so nobody gets left behind, and nobody's bored.

Resonance

Sound

Crystal singing bowls, chimes, and voice, played over cool down & savasana. The best part, in our opinion.

The shape of it

Inside a 60-minute Sol Flow event.

Our standard format, start to finish. Guests arrive in whatever they're wearing, and leave having done something they'll remember.

0–10Minutes

Dharma talk & meditation

A short talk to set the intention for the hour, usually themed to why your group gathered, followed by a guided meditation to settle in. No experience required.

10–45Minutes

Flow

The movement portion: vinyasa for groups with energy to spend, yin for groups who need the opposite. Every pose is cued with an option that works from a chair.

45–60Minutes

Cool down, savasana & sound bath

We slow the body down, then settle into savasana under crystal bowls and chimes. Low light and fifteen minutes where nobody has anywhere to be.

60 minutes is our standard. We run a 45-minute express format for weekday offsites, and 2-hour and half-day formats for retreats and wellness days.
